# Northwind Billing Assistant — intent labelling guidelines

Label each customer message with exactly one intent.

## billing_dispute
The customer says a charge on the bill is wrong or unexpected and wants it investigated.

## payment_failed
The customer reports a card or bank payment that did not go through.

## other
Anything that is not a billing intent (greetings, outage reports, unrelated questions).
