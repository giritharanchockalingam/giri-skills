# Account Planning Method

## 1. Snapshot
| Field | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue by period (last 4–8 quarters) | CRM/finance export with date | Never from memory |
| Active engagements: scope, value, end date, delivery health | Engagement list; delivery lead statement | End dates drive renewal risk |
| Contract vehicles (MSA, rate card validity, preferred-supplier status) | Legal/commercial | Expiry dates are risks |
| Health signals (NPS, escalations, reference status) | Delivery / client statements | Cite the source and date |
| Our team on the account (roles, allocation) | User | — |

## 2. Stakeholder mapping
| Field | Values | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Influence | Decision-maker · Influencer · Gatekeeper · User · Unknown | From org knowledge or discovery; not inferred from title alone where it matters |
| Disposition | Advocate · Neutral · Skeptic · Unknown | Requires evidence (statement, behavior); default is Unknown |
| Relationship owner (ours) | Named | Every decision-maker needs one |
| Last meaningful contact | Date | "Meaningful" = two-way conversation, not a newsletter |
| Next contact plan | Action, date | — |

Coverage gaps (computed):
| Gap | Rule |
|---|---|
| Uncovered decision-maker | No relationship owner, or no contact in 90 days |
| Single-threaded | Only one of our people knows the stakeholder |
| Uncovered business unit | No stakeholder mapped in a BU with white-space signal |
| Skeptic without plan | Disposition skeptic and no play addresses it |
| Departure exposure | Advocate rumored or announced leaving; no successor relationship |

## 3. White-space map
Matrix: rows = our service lines (from the catalog); columns = client business units, platforms, or programs (from the brief/account facts).

| Cell value | Meaning | Required content |
|---|---|---|
| `current` | Active engagement | Engagement ID |
| `qualified` | Opportunity in pipeline | Opportunity ID |
| `hypothesis` | Signal suggests a need | Signal (source) + confirming action + owner |
| `no fit` | Not a plausible need or blocked | Reason (e.g., incumbent locked, policy) |
| `unknown` | No information | Discovery action |

Signal strength for hypotheses: H (client stated a need or a dated forcing event), M (public signal: posting, filing, program), L (pattern inference). Only H and M hypotheses become 90-day plays.

## 4. Account posture
| Posture | Conditions (from facts) | Plan emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Grow | Delivery healthy; advocates in ≥2 BUs; H/M white-space signals; no renewal exposure in the quarter | Expansion plays, executive access, reference development |
| Defend | Renewal within 2 quarters; competitive entry evidenced; delivery issue or skeptic in decision chain | Renewal plan, delivery recovery, stakeholder re-coverage |
| Harvest | Client strategy moving away from our services; low white-space; relationships thin | Protect margin, minimal investment, graceful exit planning |
| Enter | Target account with no active engagement | Access plays, assessment offer, reference-led introduction |

State the posture and the two or three facts that drove it. A posture without facts is a wish.

## 5. Risk patterns
| Risk | Early signal | Typical mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Renewal loss | RFP issued for current scope; procurement engaged early; rate challenge | Value review with sponsor; independent baseline of outcomes delivered; renewal proposal 2 quarters out |
| Sponsor departure | Announcement, org change, reduced access | Multi-thread now; introduce successor early |
| Competitive entry | New vendor logos in client comms; "benchmark" exercises; vendor-shaped RFP language | Battle card; seed evaluation criteria; executive outreach |
| Delivery issue | Escalations, missed milestones, change requests disputed | Recovery plan with dates; executive sponsor call; do not hide |
| Budget cut | Hiring freeze, program pauses, earnings commentary | Re-scope to cost-out plays; phase; protect run work |
| Concentration | One engagement > 60% of account revenue | Diversify BUs; smaller parallel engagements |

## 6. Play design
| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Objective | One sentence with a measurable outcome in 90 days |
| Actions | 2–5 with owner and date |
| Success measure | Observable (meeting held, proposal submitted, renewal signed, stakeholder disposition moved with evidence) |
| Addresses | White-space cell ID or risk ID |
| Investment | Hours or cost if any; sourced |

Minimum plays: one coverage play, one growth play (if posture is Grow/Enter), one defense play (if Defend), one delivery-quality play (if active engagements). Maximum six; more means none get done.

## 7. Review cadence
| Review | Cadence | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Play check | Bi-weekly | Actions done / slipped; blockers |
| Pipeline | Monthly | Stage movement; next steps; forecast changes (from CRM) |
| Plan refresh | Quarterly | Change log: achieved, dropped (why), new; posture reassessed |

Refresh output includes a change log table: `Item | Prior plan | Now | Reason`.
