# Deck Redline Rubric

Score 0–3 per criterion before and after. Target after-score: every criterion ≥ 2, total ≥ 20/24.

| # | Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Storyline | No discernible argument | Argument present, gaps | Coherent; one weak link | Coherent; titles alone tell it |
| 2 | Decision clarity | No ask | Ask implied | Ask stated late | Ask on slide 1–2 and last slide, with owners and dates |
| 3 | Title assertions | Labels | Mixed | Mostly assertions | All assertions with number or consequence, ≤12 words |
| 4 | Evidence | Claims without support | Some support, unsourced | Sourced, some gaps | Every claim sourced or `[TBD — owner]` |
| 5 | So-what | Absent | In speaker's head | Present but buried | First line of every body |
| 6 | Density | Walls of text | Heavy | One point per slide mostly | One point per slide; ≤60 words; tables ≤7 rows |
| 7 | Numbers discipline | Invented or conflicting | Unsourced | Mostly sourced | All sourced or marked; reconciled across slides |
| 8 | Time fit | 2× over slot | 1.5× | Slight over, appendix used | Fits; appendix carries detail |

## Redline table layout
| Slide # | Original title | Verdict | Reason | New title (assertion) | Body change (summary) | Source / `[TBD]` items | Moves to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Verdicts: `[KEEP]` `[SHARPEN]` `[CUT]` `[ADD]` `[TBD]` (a slide may carry `[SHARPEN]` + `[TBD]`).

## Rewritten slide layout (per slide)
```
Slide n — <Assertion title>
So what: <one line>
Body:
<table or ≤60 words or chart spec: type, x, y, series, source>
Source: <doc/page or [TO BE BASELINED — owner: <role>]>
Notes: <≤60 words>
```
