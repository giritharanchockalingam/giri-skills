# Questions to Ask (by interviewer)

Pick 6–8 per interview. Ask the ones whose answers change your decision. Never ask what the website answers.

## Recruiter
| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| What is the compensation band and the bonus/equity structure for this role? | Avoid wasted rounds |
| Why is the role open, and how long has it been open? | Reposting and churn signal |
| What does the interview process look like, and who makes the final decision? | Plan effort |
| Is relocation assistance offered? What is the remote/onsite policy in practice? | Logistics |

## Hiring manager
| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| What must be true in 12 months for you to say this hire succeeded? | Reveals the real mandate |
| What has the previous leader (or the interim) struggled with? | Hidden problems |
| What are the team's size, structure, and open roles today? | Scope reality check |
| What budget does this role own, and how is it decided each year? | Level and authority |
| Which decisions can I make alone, and which need your sign-off? | Decision rights |
| How do you prefer to be kept informed? | Operating cadence |

## Panel — peers
| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| What would make this role a great partner to yours? | Partnership expectations |
| Where do technology and the business most often disagree here? | Conflict map |
| What is one thing you would change about how IT works with you? | Honest pain points |

## Panel — direct reports / architects
| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| What is the team most proud of in the last year? What is most frustrating? | Morale and blockers |
| How much of the work is run vs. change today? | Capacity reality |
| Where is technical debt slowing you down most? | Priorities |

## CIO / CTO
| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| What are your top three priorities for the next 18 months, and where does this role sit in them? | Alignment |
| How does the executive team view technology — cost center, enabler, or product? | Culture |
| What is the technology budget trajectory? | Funding reality |
| What has the board asked about technology recently? | Governance exposure |
| How do you define success for your direct reports at 90 days and at one year? | Expectations |

## CEO / business executive
| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| What business outcome is technology most on the hook for this year? | Mandate |
| What is the biggest bet the company is making, and how does technology support it? | Strategy |
| What would cause you to lose confidence in a technology leader? | Risk map |

## Questions not to ask
- Vacation, hours, perks (save for offer stage).
- Anything answered on the careers page or in the JD.
- "Do you have any concerns about my candidacy?" — instead: "Is there anything I can clarify about my experience with X?"
