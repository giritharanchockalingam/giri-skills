# Decision Matrix

Default weights (sum = 100). The user may override; record the weights used.

| # | Criterion | Default weight | What a 5 looks like | What a 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | COL-adjusted total compensation | 25 | ≥ 15% above current adjusted | Below current |
| D2 | Role scope and authority | 15 | Owns budget, org, roadmap; reports to CIO/CTO | Dotted-line, no budget |
| D3 | Career trajectory | 15 | Clear path to VP/CIO; visible to execs | Lateral, capped |
| D4 | Company health and stability | 10 | Growing, funded, low layoff risk | Restructuring, losses |
| D5 | Location / remote / travel fit | 10 | Matches user's constraints | Requires unwanted move or travel |
| D6 | Manager and culture signals | 10 | Clear mandate, decision rights, candor in interviews | Vague, high churn |
| D7 | Family and personal timing | 10 | Fits school year, family plans | Conflicts |
| D8 | Learning and domain interest | 5 | Industry and problems the user wants | Industry on avoid-list |

## Scoring
- Scale 1–5 per criterion; cite the evidence for each score in one phrase.
- `weighted = Σ (weight × score) / 5` → 0–100.
- Show the table for every option, including "stay in current role" where applicable.
- A difference under 5 points is a tie; break it on D1 after negotiation or on D7.

## Kill criteria (fail any → do not accept regardless of score)
- Written title/reporting line differs from what was discussed.
- Non-compete would block the user's realistic next move.
- Relocation required before the user's stated earliest date.
- Comp below the user's stated floor after negotiation.
