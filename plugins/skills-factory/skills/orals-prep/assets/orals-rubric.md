# Orals Dry-Run Rubric

Score each presenter and the team 0–3 per criterion after every dry run. Target before the real session: no criterion below 2; team total ≥ 24/30.

## Team criteria
| # | Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Timing | >20% over or under | 10–20% off | Within 10% | On time; close delivered in full |
| T2 | Evaluation coverage | Criteria missed | Some covered | All covered | All covered with a proof each, in the evaluators' terms |
| T3 | Opening and close | Missing or generic | Present, weak | Clear ask | Their situation + outcome + ask; memorable sentence |
| T4 | Handoffs | Confused | Awkward | Clean | Scripted and natural; each speaker's credibility set up |
| T5 | Number discipline | New or contradicting numbers | One slip | Clean | Clean; sources cited aloud where it matters |
| T6 | Q&A answers | Rambling, defensive | Mixed | ≤75 words mostly | Direct, proof, stop; unknowns handled with "we will send by <date>" |
| T7 | Hard three | Avoided or spun | Partial | Honest with mitigation | Honest, concise, mitigation with owner; lands with evaluators |
| T8 | Competitor and reference hygiene | Competitor named / uncleared client | One slip | Clean | Clean; ghost lines used naturally |
| T9 | Role credibility | Wrong person answering | Some mismatch | Mostly right | The person who will do the work answers about the work |
| T10 | Presence | Reading slides; filler | Some | Engaged | Eye contact, pauses, silence after answers, no filler |
