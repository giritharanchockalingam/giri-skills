# Executive Résumé Structure (Sr Director / VP / CxO IT)

## Section order and length
| # | Section | Length | Content rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Header | 2 lines | Name, target title line, city/state (or "Remote"), phone, email, LinkedIn. No street address. |
| 2 | Executive summary | ≤4 lines | Level, domain, scale (people/$), 2–3 signature outcomes with numbers from MR, 1 line on what you bring to this JD. No adjectives. |
| 3 | Core competencies | 9–12 phrases | JD-mapped keyword phrases. ATS-safe separators. |
| 4 | Selected achievements (optional) | 3–4 bullets | Only if the MR has 3+ headline metrics; otherwise skip |
| 5 | Professional experience | 1.3 pages | Reverse chronological. Last 15 years detailed; older roles as one line each. Each role: 1-line scope statement (team, budget, reporting line) + 3–6 bullets. |
| 6 | Education & certifications | 2–4 lines | Degree, institution; certifications with year if in MR |
| 7 | Board / advisory / speaking (optional) | ≤3 lines | Only if in MR |

Total: 2 pages. Three pages only for CxO with 25+ years and board roles.

## What to cut at executive level
- Technical minutiae older than 10 years (languages, versions)
- "References available on request"
- Objective statements
- Soft-skill lists ("team player", "excellent communicator")
- Every role before year 15 reduced to `Title, Employer, Years`

## Scope statement template (per role)
`<Title> reporting to <CIO/CTO/SVP>. <N> direct / <M> total staff, $<X>M budget, <portfolio description>. [MR-Lxx]`
All values must come from the MR; omit the element if the MR does not state it.

## Level signals the reader looks for
| Signal | Where to show it |
|---|---|
| Budget ownership | Scope statement, summary |
| Org size and structure | Scope statement |
| Reporting line | Scope statement |
| Multi-year transformation | First bullet of the relevant role |
| Vendor / contract outcomes | One bullet per role where MR supports |
| Executive stakeholder exposure | Summary and one bullet |
| Business outcome (revenue, cost, risk, speed) | Every bullet's tail |
