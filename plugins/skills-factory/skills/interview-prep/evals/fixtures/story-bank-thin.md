## SB-01 — Claims platform modernization (C4 Transformation, C1 Strategy, C6 Influence)
- Source: MR-L12
- S: Lakeshore Health Plans, 2.4M members; claims ran on a 1990s mainframe with 71% auto-adjudication and rising maintenance cost.
- T: Accountable for a 3-year modernization to a cloud-hosted core with no disruption to monthly claim cycles.
- A: (1) Sequenced by line of business starting with the lowest-risk plan to build credibility; (2) ran dual processing for two cycles and published variance daily; (3) replaced the original SI after year one when milestones slipped twice.
- R: Auto-adjudication 71% → 88%; mainframe retired 4 months after plan; $3M annual maintenance avoided.
- R+: Should have set SI exit criteria in the contract from day one; now standard in every program I run.

## SB-02 — Managed-services renegotiation (C5 Vendor, C2 Budget)
- Source: MR-L15
- S: Managed-services contract at $11M/yr auto-renewing with weak SLAs and no credits.
- T: Renegotiate within 90 days before auto-renewal.
- A: (1) Benchmarked rates with two alternative bids; (2) separated run work from change work so the vendor priced each; (3) traded a longer term for SLA credits and a termination-for-convenience clause.
- R: $2.6M annual reduction; SLA credits triggered twice in year one, recovering $180K.
- R+: Bring procurement in earlier; their benchmark data shortened the cycle by weeks.
