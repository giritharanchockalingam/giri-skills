## SB-01 — Claims platform modernization (C4 Transformation, C1 Strategy, C6 Influence)
- Source: MR-L12
- S: Lakeshore Health Plans, 2.4M members; claims ran on a 1990s mainframe with 71% auto-adjudication and rising maintenance cost.
- T: Accountable for a 3-year modernization to a cloud-hosted core with no disruption to monthly claim cycles.
- A: (1) Sequenced by line of business starting with the lowest-risk plan to build credibility; (2) ran dual processing for two cycles and published variance daily; (3) replaced the original SI after year one when milestones slipped twice.
- R: Auto-adjudication 71% → 88%; mainframe retired 4 months after plan; $3M annual maintenance avoided.
- R+: Should have set SI exit criteria in the contract from day one; now standard in every program I run.

## SB-02 — Managed-services renegotiation (C5 Vendor, C2 Budget)
- Source: MR-L15
- S: Managed-services contract at $11M/yr auto-renewing with weak SLAs and no credits.
- T: Renegotiate within 90 days before auto-renewal.
- A: (1) Benchmarked rates with two alternative bids; (2) separated run work from change work so the vendor priced each; (3) traded a longer term for SLA credits and a termination-for-convenience clause.
- R: $2.6M annual reduction; SLA credits triggered twice in year one, recovering $180K.
- R+: Bring procurement in earlier; their benchmark data shortened the cycle by weeks.

## SB-03 — ERP consolidation (C4 Transformation, C2 Budget)
- Source: MR-L13
- S: Three ERP instances from acquisitions, three charts of accounts, month-end close at 12 days.
- T: Consolidate onto one S/4HANA tenant inside the existing budget envelope.
- A: (1) Froze customizations and adopted standard processes with CFO sponsorship; (2) ran a data-quality program six months ahead of migration; (3) closed two data centers to fund the program.
- R: One tenant, close at 6 days, two data centers closed; delivered within budget.
- R+: Business process owners should have been named before vendor selection, not after.

## SB-04 — FinOps program (C2 Budget, C8 Operations)
- Source: MR-L14
- S: Azure spend growing 30% a year with no ownership by workload.
- T: Cut spend without slowing delivery.
- A: (1) Tagged every workload to an owner and showed them the bill monthly; (2) rightsized and reserved capacity; (3) set a cloud budget per product team with reinvestment rights for savings.
- R: 23% reduction ($4.2M) in 18 months; reinvested $1M into security tooling.
- R+: Transparency drove more savings than any technical lever.

## SB-05 — Restructuring the application org (C3 Org leadership, C10 Change)
- Source: MR-L10, MR-L11
- S: 120-person org organized by technology layer; business partners had no single owner for their outcomes.
- T: Move to product-aligned teams without losing delivery during open enrollment.
- A: (1) Designed four product lines with named leads; (2) staffed changes after enrollment peak; (3) ran monthly skip-levels for the first two quarters to catch attrition risk early.
- R: Regretted attrition under 5% through the change; cycle time for business requests down 35%.
- R+: Announce the end state and the timeline together; ambiguity between them cost us two good engineers.

## SB-06 — Board audit committee security presentation (C6 Influence, C7 Risk)
- Source: MR-L16
- S: Audit committee concerned after an industry breach; asked for a security posture review.
- T: Present a credible assessment and an investment ask in 20 minutes.
- A: (1) Framed posture against a recognized framework with a maturity score; (2) presented three options with cost and residual risk; (3) recommended the middle option with a quarterly reporting cadence.
- R: $2.4M approved; quarterly reporting adopted as standing practice.
- R+: Lead with the decision and residual risk; the committee did not want the technical appendix.

## SB-07 — Failure: policy administration slip (C4, C10)
- Source: MR-L21
- S: Guidewire implementation at Granite Mutual, first release 5 months late.
- T: Recover the program and credibility with the business.
- A: (1) Stopped and re-baselined with the business instead of promising a new date; (2) cut scope to one state for the first release; (3) moved to biweekly demos with the underwriting VP.
- R: First release shipped 7 months after re-baseline; time-to-quote 11 min → 90 s on launch state; remaining states followed over 14 months.
- R+: I had accepted an aggressive date I did not believe. I now put my own estimate on record before accepting any program date.

## SB-08 — API partner program (C1 Strategy, C9 Data/AI adjacent)
- Source: MR-L22
- S: Agency partners integrated via file transfers; onboarding took 10 weeks.
- T: Build a partner API program with no new headcount.
- A: (1) Stood up an API gateway with self-service onboarding; (2) prioritized the five highest-volume partner workflows; (3) published usage metrics to sales leadership monthly.
- R: 25 partners live in year one; onboarding 10 weeks → 2 weeks.
- R+: Product ownership for APIs should have sat with the business sooner.
