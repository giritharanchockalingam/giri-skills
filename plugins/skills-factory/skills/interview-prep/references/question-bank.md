# Question Bank by Round Type

Pair each selected question with a story ID or a GAP note in the prep output.

## Round 1 — Recruiter screen (20–30 min)
| Question | What they are checking | Answer shape |
|---|---|---|
| Walk me through your background. | Level, trajectory, clarity | 90-second arc: scale → signature outcomes → why this role |
| Why are you looking / why did you leave X? | Risk, stability | One factual sentence per move; no negativity |
| What are your compensation expectations? | Band fit | Range anchored to market data; ask for their band first |
| Location, travel, start date? | Logistics | Direct answers; constraints stated once |
| What size team and budget have you managed? | Level calibration | Numbers from MR |
| Why this company? | Preparation | Two sourced facts about their strategy |

## Round 2 — Hiring manager (45–60 min)
| Question | Competency | Answer shape |
|---|---|---|
| Tell me about a transformation you led end to end. | C4 | STAR+R with before/after KPI |
| How do you build and manage a budget? Give an example of a hard trade-off. | C2 | Portfolio-level decision, what was cut, why |
| Describe a time you had to restructure a team. | C3 | Decision, how people were treated, outcome |
| Tell me about a vendor relationship that went wrong. | C5 | Governance you installed, outcome |
| How do you decide what not to do? | C1 | Prioritization framework with one real example |
| Tell me about a failure. | C10 | Real failure, measurable recovery, lesson in use |
| What would your first 90 days look like here? | C1/C6 | Listen → baseline → align → decide; reference the plan |
| How do you work with peers who disagree with you? | C6 | Specific disagreement, how resolved |

## Round 3 — Panel (peers, direct reports, business partners; 60 min)
| Question | Asked by | Answer shape |
|---|---|---|
| How will you partner with my function? | Business VP | Their outcomes first, then your operating model |
| How do you handle a request from the business that conflicts with the roadmap? | Peer | Decision rights, transparency, example |
| What is your management style? | Direct report | Cadence, delegation, growth; one example |
| How do you keep an offshore/vendor team accountable? | Peer | Metrics, governance, relationship |
| How technical are you? | Architect | Honest level; where you go deep and where you delegate |
| How do you handle production incidents at 2 a.m.? | Ops lead | Role in incident command, post-incident practice |
| Tell us about a time you changed your mind. | Anyone | Real example, what evidence changed it |

## Round 4 — CIO / CEO (30–45 min)
| Question | What they are checking | Answer shape |
|---|---|---|
| What is your assessment of our technology position? | Strategic judgement | 3 sourced observations, 2 hypotheses, questions you would ask first |
| Where would you invest and where would you cut? | Business framing | Tie to their stated priorities; name trade-offs |
| How do you measure your organization? | Operating rigor | 5–7 metrics with targets |
| What do you need from me to succeed? | Self-awareness | Decision rights, air cover, access |
| How do you handle a board or audit committee? | Executive presence | Example from MR/SB |
| What would make you leave? | Retention risk | Honest, non-defensive |
| What is the biggest risk you see in this role? | Judgement | Name it, mitigation |

## Round 5 — Board-style case (60–90 min, often with pre-read)
Typical prompt: "You inherit a $40M portfolio, 30% over budget, a stalled ERP program and a vendor in dispute. Present your 90-day plan to the executive committee."

| Element | Expectation |
|---|---|
| Structure | Exec summary slide, situation assessment, options with trade-offs, recommendation, 90-day plan, risks, asks |
| Assumptions | Listed explicitly; each marked as assumption |
| Numbers | Only from the case pack; no invented figures |
| Decision | A clear recommendation with a kill criterion |
| Delivery | 15 minutes, then 30 of challenge; concede valid points, hold on evidence |

Challenge questions to rehearse: "Why not just cancel it?", "What if the vendor walks?", "Where does the money come from?", "Who do you fire?", "What would you do with half the budget?"
