# Message Types — Rules

Universal tone rules: peer-to-peer, direct, specific, warm without flattery. One ask per message. First sentence states why you are writing. No "I hope this finds you well", no "just checking in", no exclamation marks, no apologizing for writing.

| Type | Goal | Length | Must include | Must avoid | Variant strategies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Referral ask | Get a named insider to refer or introduce | LinkedIn ≤300 chars (connect) / email ≤150 words | Specific role + req ID, why you (1 sourced outcome), the exact ask, easy out ("if it's not a fit, no worries") | Attaching résumé unasked, multiple roles, guilt | **Direct ask** (fast, risks a no) · **Advice-first** (15-min call about the team; slower, builds relationship) · **Mutual-contact bridge** (via shared connection) |
| Recruiter reply | Advance to a screen with your constraints on record | ≤120 words | Interest level, 2 availability windows, comp expectation as a range or request for their band, location/travel constraint in one line | Over-explaining tenure, listing all skills, enthusiasm inflation | **Lead with fit** (strong role match) · **Qualify first** (ask band/location before committing time) · **Redirect** (not this role; what you would consider) |
| Post-interview thank-you | Reinforce one point, fix one gap, keep momentum | ≤120 words, within 24 h, one per interviewer | Reference one specific thing they said, one value point you bring to it, next step as you understand it | Generic gratitude, restating résumé, new asks | **Reinforce** (strongest point) · **Repair** (clarify a weak answer in 2 sentences) · **Bridge** (connect their stated priority to your 90-day thinking) |
| Status check | Get a decision or a date without seeming anxious | ≤90 words | Date of last contact, the timeline they gave, a reason to write now (new info, competing timeline, availability), explicit question | "Just following up", pressure, ultimatums (except the competing-offer variant) | **Light touch** (new information + question) · **Timeline** (competing process, need a date) · **Value add** (share a relevant article/insight, then ask) |
| Decline | Exit cleanly and keep the door open | ≤100 words | Thank specific person, one honest reason category (role scope, comp, timing, location), willingness to stay in touch | Detailed critique, bluffing, negotiation disguised as decline | **Clean close** · **Door open** (specific future scenario) · **Refer** (suggest a contact if appropriate) |
| Negotiation open | Start the comp conversation from a position of partnership | ≤150 words | Enthusiasm for the role, the gap stated as a number or lever, market/context justification, a proposed next step (call) | Ultimatums, multiple asks at once, apologizing for asking | **Base first** (single biggest lever) · **Package** (base + sign-on + start date bundled) · **Non-cash** (title, scope, remote, review timing) |

## Channel rules
| Channel | Use for | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn connection note | Cold or warm first touch | 300 characters |
| LinkedIn InMail / message | Known contacts, recruiters | 150 words |
| Email | Anything after first contact; thank-yous; negotiation | 180 words |
| Text | Only if they texted first; scheduling only | 2 sentences |

## Level signals (Sr Director / VP)
- Cite scale once (team, budget, outcome) — not a list.
- Speak about their business problem before your background.
- Offer time windows; do not ask "when works for you?".
- Sign with name and one-line title; no inspirational quotes.
