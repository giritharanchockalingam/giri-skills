# Follow-up Cadence

Business days. "Stop" means stop initiating; respond if they reach out.

| Situation | Touch 1 | Touch 2 | Touch 3 | Stop rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Referral ask (warm contact) | Day 0 LinkedIn or email | Day 5 same channel, add one new detail | Day 12 different channel | Stop after 3; thank and move on |
| Referral ask (cold contact) | Day 0 connection note | Day 7 InMail if accepted | — | Stop after 2 |
| Recruiter inbound | Reply within 1 business day | — | — | Not applicable |
| Applied, no response | Day 7 referral or recruiter nudge | Day 14 hiring-manager LinkedIn note | — | Stop after 2; mark "Ghosted" at day 30 |
| Post-interview thank-you | Within 24 h | — | — | One per interviewer |
| Awaiting decision (timeline given) | Timeline date + 2 | +7 | — | Stop after 2; move to "Stalled" |
| Awaiting decision (no timeline) | Day 5 ask for timeline | Day 12 | Day 20 | Stop after 3 |
| Competing offer in hand | Immediately, state the deadline | Deadline − 2 | — | Decide at deadline |
| Offer received, negotiating | Within 2 days: thank + request call | Call | Written confirmation same day | — |
| After decline (yours or theirs) | Day 0 clean close | Day 90 light touch (article, congratulations) | Every 90–120 days | Keep as network contact |

## Escalation ladder (applied, no response)
1. Recruiter who posted the role.
2. Referrer, asking them to ping internally.
3. Hiring manager on LinkedIn with a 2-sentence value note.
4. Stop. Log in tracker as "Ghosted" with date.

## Timing rules
- Send Tue–Thu, 7:30–9:30 a.m. recipient local time, or within 10 minutes of their last message.
- Never follow up on a Monday morning or Friday afternoon.
- Each follow-up adds one new element (news, availability change, relevant insight). Never resend the same note.
- Count touches across channels, not per channel.

## Tracker integration
After each touch, update tracker.csv: `last_touch`, `next_action`, `next_action_date`. The `application-tracker` aging rules read these fields.
